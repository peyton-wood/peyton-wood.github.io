ThreatLocker

Role Senior Designer · Deliverables Illustration · Socials · Booths · Year 2025

Bearly Awake Coffee Co. is a small coffee stand located on Whidbey Island in the Pacific Northwest. The owner came to me with a clear vision — she wanted a brand that felt fun, modern, and full of personality. The challenge was to create a visual identity that felt welcoming without leaning into the typical rustic and nature-based aesthetics of the category. The result is a bold, character-driven brand built around a sleepy bear who loves coffee just as much as the rest of us.

Social Media

Working closely with our Social Media Manager and my Creative Director, I created a visual brand for our social media posts. This look set us apart from our more educational content, which had a very pen-to-paper look. The glass reflects our company voice of innovation and disruption. The look helped set us apart from competitors by creating a very clean yet complex style.

When designing social media posts for our CEO, I opted for a simple and clean design. This created an elevated look for his social media, and helped our initiative to position him in thought-leadership.

Illustrations

The main specialization I took on in my role was illustrations. Working closely with various teams, including marketing and our C-suite, I was able to develop a style unique to our brand. The sketchy look of the illustrations called back to our goals to position the brand as educational. Our goal as a company was to teach the world about Zero Trust, so these blueprint-esque illustration referenced educational diagrams and scientific illustration. Built to work on everything, from documents to the web, these illustrations were designed in isometric perspective to create depth and interest on a flat screen or page.

Car Wraps

A new marketing initiative included branded Explorers, which traveled the country as a form of mobile billboard. Consulting marketing and our CEO, I created a series of wraps that felt fast, interesting, and secure. These vehicles have been used in numerous social media initiatives, and have been spotted by prospects from Florida to California.

Documents

A key part of our customer outreach included customer case studies and testimonials. Working closely with our copywriters and customer coordinators, we created a series of case study documents sharing the customer story. Other documents included sales product one-pagers and whitepapers.

Bearly Awake

Role Brand Designer · Deliverables Logo · Packaging · Print · Year 2024

Bearly Awake Coffee Co. is a small coffee stand on Whidbey Island in the Pacific Northwest. The owner wanted a brand that felt fun, modern, and full of personality. The result is a bold, character-driven brand built around a sleepy bear who loves coffee just as much as the rest of us.

Logo Suite

The logo suite was designed to work across every surface. The primary logo leads with the bear mascot and a mug. The secondary mark strips it back for smaller applications. The wordmark gives flexibility in typographic contexts like menus and signage.

Brand Colors

Most PNW coffee brands lean into natural tones. We went the opposite direction — teal nodding to Whidbey Island's waters, bright green for energy, and pink as for an unexpected pop makes every touchpoint impossible to ignore.
